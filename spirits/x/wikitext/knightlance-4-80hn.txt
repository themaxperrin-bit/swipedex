}}{{Translation|'''KnightLance 4-80HN'''|ナイトランス{{Ruby|4-80|フォーエイティー}}{{Ruby|HN|ハイニードル}}|NaitoRansu Fō Eitī Hai Nīdoru|KnightLance Four Eighty High Needle}} is a Defense Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Booster in Japan on August 10th, 2023 for 1400円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Lance Knight 4-80HN''' in a Starter Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$9.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[KnightShield 3-80N]] and is succeeded by [[KnightMail 3-85BS]].
