It was released as a part of the [[Customize Set U]] in Japan on November 2nd, 2024 for 5700円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Mail Knight 3-85BS''' as part of the [[X-treme Expansion Pack]] released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$29.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[KnightLance 4-80HN]] and is succeeded by [[KnightFortress GV8-70UN]].

==Trivia==
*"''Mail''" is likely referring to [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain_mail chain ''mail''], a type of armor consisting of small metal rings linked together in a pattern to form a mesh.
**This is supported by the 4X Avatar's method of defending by using the armor to block attacks.
*KnightMail's avatar carries both a shield and a lance, which are both suffices to its [[KnightShield 3-80N|predec]][[KnightLance 4-80HN|essors]].
