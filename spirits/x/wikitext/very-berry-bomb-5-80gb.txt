}}{{Translation|'''Very Berry Bomb 5-80GB'''|ベリーベリーボム5-80GB|Berī Berī Bomu 5-80GB|Very Berry Bomb Five Eighty Gear Ball}} or '''Very Berry Cherry 5-80GB''' is a Stamina Type Beyblade that is owned by [[Number One]] in the ''[[Beyblade X]]'' anime.

==Blade - Very Berry Bomb==
'''Very Berry Bomb''' is the [[Blade - WyvernGale|WyvernGale]] Blade with a different Gear Chip sticker attached. The Gear Chip features the Blade's namesake, the [[wikipedia:Strawberry|Berry]] fruit.
