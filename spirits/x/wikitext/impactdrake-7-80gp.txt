}}{{Translation|'''ImpactDrake 7-80GP'''|インパクトドレイク{{Ruby|7-80|セブンエイティー}}{{Ruby|GP|ギヤポイント}}|InpakutoDoreiku Sebun Eitī Giya Pointo|ImpactDrake Seven Eighty Gear Point}} is a Balance Type Beyblade that appears in ''[[Beyblade X (Season 2)|Beyblade X]]''. As an anime-exclusive Beyblade, it was not released.

However, this toy can be created with the [[Blade - ImpactDrake|ImpactDrake]] Blade from Takara Tomy's [[ImpactDrake 9-60LR|ImpactDrake]] Starter or Hasbro's [[Drop Attack Battle Set]] and the [[Ratchet - 7-80|7-80]] Ratchet and [[Bit - Gear Point|Gear Point]] Bit from Takara Tomy's [[Random Booster ShelterDrake Select]] or Hasbro's [[ShelterDrake 7-80GP|Shelter Drake 7-80GP]] Starter Pack.

It uses the parts of [[ShelterDrake 7-80GP]], making it the Balance custom of [[ImpactDrake 9-60LR]].
