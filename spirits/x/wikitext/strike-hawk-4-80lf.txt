In the anime, Robin's Strike Hawk was given to him by Tsubasa Takano.

==Blade - Strike Hawk==
In the anime, '''Strike Hawk''' is the [[Blade - WizardArrow|WizardArrow]] Blade with a different Gear Chip sticker attached. The Gear Chip features the Blade's namesake, the [[wikipedia:Hawk|Hawk]], a bird of prey.

In the manga, the '''Strike Hawk''' Blade has three blades acting as the main contact points. The Gear Chip features a hawk, with "ストライク", the Katakana for "strike" written on it.
