}}{{Translation|'''SharkEdge 1-60Q'''|シャークエッジ{{Ruby|1-60|ワンシクスティー}}{{Ruby|Q|クエイク}}|ShākuEjji Wan Shikusutī Kueiku|SharkEdge One Sixty Quake}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released in Japan on April 27th, 2024 for 1400円 as one of six Beyblades in [[Random Booster Vol. 3]]. It was released by [[Hasbro]] as '''Keel Shark 1-60Q''' in a Booster Pack for USD$7.99 in the United States.
