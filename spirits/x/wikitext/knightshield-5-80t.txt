It was released as a part of the [[DranDagger Deck Set]] in Japan on November 2nd, 2023 for 3960円. It was released by [[Hasbro]] as '''Helm Knight 5-80T''' in the [[Yell Kong and Helm Knight Dual Pack]] for USD$14.99 in the United States.
