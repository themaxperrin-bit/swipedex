It was released in Japan on December 27th, 2023 for 1400円 as one of six possible Beyblades in [[Random Booster Vol. 2]]. It was released by [[Hasbro]] as '''Arrow Wizard 4-80GB''' in a Booster Pack for USD$7.99 in the United States.
