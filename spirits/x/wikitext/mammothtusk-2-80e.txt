}}{{Translation|'''MammothTusk 2-80E'''|マンモスタスク{{Ruby|2-80|ツーエイティー}}{{Ruby|E|エレベート}}|ManmosuTasuku Tsū Eitī Erebēto|MammothTusk Two Eighty Elevate}} is a Balance Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]] in Japan for 1600円.

It was first released in Japan as an exclusive to attendees of the [[Beyblade Park]] exhibit, which opened on December 27th, 2024. It was later re-released as part of the [[Rare Bey Get Battle]] in Japan on March 27th, 2025. Through the [[Beyblade X (app)|''Beyblade X'' app]], Bladers have a 3% chance of obtaining a "Limited Edition Purchase Ticket", which can be then redeemed with the local distributor.
