The three parts of the Bey were selected in a vote held by CoroCoro Comic, and was dubbed the '''Bladers Select Bey'''.<ref>CoroCoro Comic October 2024 Issue, page 11</ref> Although not shown in the manga, it appears in promotional art with [[Blaze Fujiwara]] in the October 2024 issue of [[CoroCoro Comic]].
