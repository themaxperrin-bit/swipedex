}}{{Translation|'''DranBrave S6-60V'''|ドランブレイブ{{Ruby|S|スラッシュ}}{{Ruby|6-60|シックスシックスティ}}{{Ruby|V|ボルテックス}}|DoranBureibu Surasshu Shikkusu Shikkusuti Borutekkusu|DranBrave Slash Six Sixty Vortex}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Starter in Japan on March 29th, 2025 for 2200円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Courage Dran S 6-60V''' in a Custom Line Starter Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$12.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[DranBuster 1-60A]] and is succeeded by its upgrade, [[DranBrave H6-60V]], and [[DranStrike 4-50FF]].

==Trivia==
*The Dran Lock Chip design was first erroneously revealed on the cover of ''Beyblade X'' volume 5, which placed it on [[DranBuster 1-60A]]'s Gear Chip.
*There are three variants of Hasbro's Courage Dran starter pack:
**Initial prints of the wave 5 packaging feature Courage Dran with red printed detailing rather than its normal blue ones. The renders used are likely from a prototype version of Courage Dran.
**Revised prints of the wave 5 packaging feature the normal blue detailing.
**The wave 7 reprint of the Courage Dran starter pack, with the "let it rip!" perforation removed.
*DranBrave's avatar is an upgrade version of DranSword/Dagger/Buster's with several alterations:
**Blue armor is added to the nose, neck, wrists, abdomen and tail.
**A dragon-shaped hilt is added to the sword.
**The manga illustrates the ''Dran'' avatar with a full body armor.
