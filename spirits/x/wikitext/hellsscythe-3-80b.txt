It was released as a part of the [[3on3 Deck Set]] in Japan on July 15th, 2023 for 3960円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Scythe Incendio 3-80B''' in a Booster Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$7.99 in the United States.
