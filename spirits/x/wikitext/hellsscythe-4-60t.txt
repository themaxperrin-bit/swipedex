}}{{Translation|'''HellsScythe 4-60T'''|ヘルズサイズ{{Ruby|4-60|フォーシクスティー}}{{Ruby|T|テーパー}}|HeruzuSaizu Fō Shikusutī Tēpā|HellsScythe Four Sixty Taper}} is a Balance Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Starter in Japan on June 10th, 2023 during the CoroCoro Spirit Festival for 1980円. Its regular retail release in Japan was on July 15th, 2023. It later debuted in western countries as '''Scythe Incendio 4-60T''' in a Starter Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$9.99 in the United States.

The HellsScythe Blade would later get a Version 2.0 release, which released in Japan as a part of the CX-20 [[Customize Set C]] on November 21st, 2026 for 6600円.

In the anime and manga, it was given to Robin, following the destruction of his [[Strike Hawk 4-80LF|Strike Hawk]]. In the anime, Multi's older sister, [[Sigrid Nana-iro]] originally owned the prototype version of the Beyblade. The completed version was also originally meant to be for her. <!--HellsScythe's ''Version 2.0'' release would later debut in the anime/manga series, which would be developed by the [[Bey Kingdom]].-->

It is succeeded by [[HellsChain 5-60HT]].
