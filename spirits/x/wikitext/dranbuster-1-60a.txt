It was released as a Starter in Japan on March 30th, 2024. It later debuted in western countries as '''Buster Dran 1-60A''' in a Starter Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$9.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[DranDagger 4-60R]] and is succeeded by [[DranBrave S6-60V]].

==Trivia==
* According to the flyer included with [[Random Booster SphinxCowl Select]], the UX-01 Starter was supposed to release on March 23rd, 2024, but was delayed 1 week for unknown reasons.
* The "Metal Coat: Red" release could be obtained via the May 2024 issue of the [[CoroCoro Comic]] magazine released on April 15th, 2024 in Japan. A "silver peel-off campaign" was held, with 100 winners receiving the Beyblade.<ref>[https://corocoro.jp/news/382326/ Metal Coat: Red release information]</ref> It was later rereleased together with ''Beyblade X'' manga Volume 1 Full Color Special Edition.
*An officially licensed miniaturized version of this product was developed and released by American toy company Super Impulse in 2026 under their "World's Smallest" label, alongside [[HellsHammer 3-70H]] and [[WizardRod 5-70DB]].
