}}{{Translation|'''GhostCircle 0-80GB'''|ゴーストサークル{{Ruby|0-80|ゼロエイティー}}{{Ruby|GB|ギヤボール}}|GōsutoSākuru Zero Eitī Giya Bōru|GhostCircle Zero Eighty Gear Ball}} is a Stamina Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released in Japan on December 28th, 2024 for 1400円 as the prize Beyblade in [[Random Booster Vol. 5]]. It later debuted in western countries as '''Circle Ghost 0-80GB''' as part of the [[Circle Ghost and Chain Incendio Dual Pack]] released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$14.99 in the United States.
