It was released as a part of the [[DranDagger Deck Set]] in Japan on November 2nd, 2023 for 3960円. It was released by [[Hasbro]] as '''Keel Shark 3-80F''' in the [[Knife Shinobi and Keel Shark Dual Pack]] for USD$14.99 in the United States.
