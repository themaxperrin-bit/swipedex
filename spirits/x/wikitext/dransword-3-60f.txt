}}{{Translation|'''DranSword 3-60F'''|ドランソード{{Ruby|3-60|スリーシクスティー}}{{Ruby|F|フラット}}|DoranSōdo Surī Shikusutī Furatto|DranSword Three Sixty Flat}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Starter in Japan on June 10th, 2023 during the CoroCoro Spirit Festival for 1980円. Its regular retail release in Japan was on July 15th, 2023. It later debuted in western countries as '''Sword Dran 3-60F''' in a Starter Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$9.99 in the United States.

DranSword would later get a ''Version 2.0'' release, which released in Japan as a BX-00 Booster on August 8th, 2026 for 1600円.

In the anime and manga, it was given to a young [[Jaxon Cross]] as a parting gift from his mentor, [[Ginro]]; it originally had ''Metal Coat: Blue'' paint, although this gradually wore down over years into its current form. [[Khrome Ryugu]] also borrows this Bey for his match at the peak of [[The X]], and later uses a replica bought from a store. DranSword's ''Version 2.0'' release would later debut in the anime/manga series, which would be developed by the [[Bey Kingdom]].

It is succeeded by [[DranDagger 4-60R]].

==Trivia==
*DranSword's name is based on a "dragon" and a "sword"; the Bey combines the "movement of a dragon" with the "sharpness of a sword", hence the name "DranSword".<ref name="CoroCoro July 2023">CoroCoro July 2023 Issue</ref>
*DranSword's name during development was originally "'''Dragon Dagger'''".<ref>[[Beyblade X App]] datamined file</ref>
**"Dagger" was later used for DranSword's successor, [[DranDagger 4-60R]].
*This is the only Dran Beyblade that was not crafted by Multi.
*DranSword is the first Beyblade to be released as "Version 2.0".
