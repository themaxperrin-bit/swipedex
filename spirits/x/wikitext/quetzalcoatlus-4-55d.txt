}}{{Translation|'''Quetzalcoatlus 4-55D'''|ケツァルコアトルス{{Ruby|4-55|フォーフィフティーファイブ}}{{Ruby|D|ドット}}|Ketsuarukoatorusu Fō Fifutī Faibu Dotto|Quetzalcoatlus Four Fifty Five Dot}} is a Defense Type Beyblade released by [[Hasbro]]. It was released as a part of the [[Spinosaurus and Quetzalcoatlus Multipack Set]] for USD$24.99 in the United States. It later debuted as a part of the same set released by [[Takara Tomy]] on July 19th, 2025 for 4180円 in Japan.

The Takara Tomy release refers to this Beyblade's full name as '''Quetzalcoatlus PteraSwing 4-55D Jurassic World Version'''.
