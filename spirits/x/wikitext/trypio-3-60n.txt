}}{{Translation|'''Trypio 3-60N'''|トライピオ3-60N|Toraipio 3-60N|Trypio Three Sixty Needle}} is a Defense Type Beyblade and the ''[[Beyblade X]]'' remake of the [[Spin Gear System]] Beyblade [[Trypio]].

In an August 2023 poll conducted by [[Takara Tomy]] for the [[X-Over Project]], Trypio was voted as the #1 finalist. Two prototypes were developed, with the first one being unveiled at the ''X-Treme Cup'' G1 2023 event on December 9th, 2023.<ref>https://twitter.com/tbh_pr/status/1734043627796811908</ref>

However, on January 29th, 2024 the development team announced that Trypio remake had been canceled due to various safety issues, along with concerns about the marketability of the product.<ref>https://www.beach.jp/circleboard/af10964/topictree/1100217168165</ref> A major concern was that a Blader's fingers could get entangled in the Trypio Blade during a [[Launch]], due to the wide diameter of Trypio's design. While a second prototype with a smaller diameter was considered, designers felt that this design deviated too much from the original look and feel of Trypio, so ultimately shelved the release entirely.
