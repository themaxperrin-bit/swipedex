}}{{Translation|'''PhoenixWing 9-60GF'''|フェニックスウイング{{Ruby|9-60|ナインシクスティー}}{{Ruby|GF|ギヤフラット}}|FenikkusuUingu Nain Shikusutī Giya Furatto|PhoenixWing Nine Sixty Gear Flat}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Starter in Japan on December 10th, 2023 as a promotional Toys R' Us pre-sale for 2420円. Its regular retail release in Japan was on December 27th, 2023 for 2420円.

It later debuted in western countries as '''Soar Phoenix 9-60GF''' in the '''Soar Phoenix Deluxe String Launcher Set''' released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$19.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[Blaze Fujiwara]]'s [[PhoenixFeather 3-60F]] and is succeeded by [[PhoenixRudder 9-70G]].
