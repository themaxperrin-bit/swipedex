It was released as a Starter in Japan on March 30th, 2024. It was released by [[Hasbro]] as '''Hammer Incendio 3-70H''' in a Starter Pack for USD$9.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[HellsChain 5-60HT]] and is succeeded by [[HellsReaper T4-70K]].

==Trivia==
*The packaging of the initial Hasbro starter pack release has erroneously labeled the Blade as an Attack type. This was fixed in the wave 7 reprint.
*The wave 7 reprint of the Hasbro starter pack has the "let it rip!" perforation removed.
*An officially licensed miniaturized version of this product was developed and released by American toy company Super Impulse in 2026 under their "World's Smallest" label, alongside [[DranBuster 1-60A]] and [[WizardRod 5-70DB]].
