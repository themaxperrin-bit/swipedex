}}{{Translation|'''Stone Mont Blanc 3-80N'''|ストーンモンブラン3-80N|Sutōn Mon Buran 3-80N|Stone Mont Blanc Three Eighty Needle}} or '''Stone Mountain 3-80N''' is a Defense Type Beyblade that is owned by [[Takumi Ishiyama]] in the ''[[Beyblade X]]'' anime and manga.

Although this Beyblade did not receive a full release from [[Takara Tomy]], an official tournament legal Gear Chip sticker was distributed at [[Beyblade Battle Base]] (B4) events.

==Blade - Stone Mont Blanc==
In the anime, '''Stone Mont Blanc''' is the [[Blade - KnightShield|KnightShield]] Blade with a different Gear Chip sticker attached. The Gear Chip features the Blade's namesake, [[wikipedia:Mont Blanc|Mont Blanc]], the highest mountain in the Alps.

In the manga, the '''Stone Mont Blanc''' Blade has a round shape, with six protrusions. The Gear Chip features a stone head, with two smaller stones next to it.
