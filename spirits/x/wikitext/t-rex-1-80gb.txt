}}{{Translation|'''T. Rex 1-80GB'''|T-レックス{{Ruby|1-80|ワンーエイティー}}{{Ruby|GB|ギヤボール}}|T-Rekkusu Wan Eitī Giya Bōru|T. Rex One Eighty Gear Ball}} is a Stamina Type Beyblade released by [[Hasbro]]. It was released as a part of the [[T. Rex and Mosasaurus Multipack Set]] for USD$24.99 in the United States. It later debuted as a part of the same set released by [[Takara Tomy]] on July 19th, 2025 for 4180円 in Japan.

The Takara Tomy release refers to this Beyblade's full name as '''T. Rex TyrannoBeat 1-80GB Jurassic World Version'''.
