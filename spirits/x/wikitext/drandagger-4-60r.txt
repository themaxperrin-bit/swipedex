}}{{Translation|'''DranDagger 4-60R'''|ドランダガー{{Ruby|4-60|フォーシクスティー}}{{Ruby|R|ラッシュ}}|DoranDagā Fō Shikusutī Rasshu|DranDagger Four Sixty Rush}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a part of the [[DranDagger Deck Set]] in Japan on November 2nd, 2023 for 3960円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Dagger Dran 4-60R''' in the [[Xtreme Battle Set]] released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$49.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[DranSword 3-60F]] and is succeeded by [[DranBuster 1-60A]].
