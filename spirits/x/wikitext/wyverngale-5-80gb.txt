}}{{Translation|'''WyvernGale 5-80GB'''|ワイバーンゲイル{{Ruby|5-80|ファイブエイティー}}{{Ruby|GB|ギヤボール}}|WaibānGeiru Faibu Eitī Giya Bōru|WyvernGale Five Eighty Gear Ball}} is a Stamina Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released in Japan on December 27th, 2023 for 1400円 as the prize Beyblade in [[Random Booster Vol. 2]]. It later debuted in western countries as '''Gale Wyvern 5-80GB''' as part of the [[Gale Wyvern and Tail Viper Dual Pack]] released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$14.99 in the United States.

It is succeeded by [[Hover Wyvern 3-85N]] in the Hasbro toyline, and [[WyvernHover 8-80B]] in the Takara Tomy toyline.
