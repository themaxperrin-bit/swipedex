==Trivia==
*Knife Shinobi is the first Bey to appear in the ''Beyblade X'' anime to be based on a Hasbro exclusive product.
*Product renders show the inner part of the 4-80 Ratchet and HN Bit in pink and purple respectively, rather than the orange and blue used in the anime and actual toy.
*Knife Shinobi shares the same stock combo as [[KnightLance 4-80HN]].
