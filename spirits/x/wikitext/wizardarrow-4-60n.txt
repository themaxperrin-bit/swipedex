}}{{Translation|'''WizardArrow 4-60N'''|ウィザードアロー{{Ruby|4-60|フォーシクスティー}}{{Ruby|N|ニードル}}|WizādoArō Fō Shikusutī Nīdoru|WizardArrow Four Sixty Needle}} is a Defense Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a part of the [[3on3 Deck Set]] in Japan on July 15th, 2023 for 3960円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Arrow Wizard 4-60N''' as part of the [[Chain Incendio and Arrow Wizard Dual Pack]] released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$14.99 in the United States.
