}}{{Translation|'''ImpactDrake 9-60LR'''|インパクトドレイク{{Ruby|9-60|ナインシクスティー}}{{Ruby|LR|ローラッシュ}}|InpakutoDoreiku Nain Shikusutī Rō Rasshu|ImpactDrake Nine Sixty Low Rush}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Starter in Japan on December 28th, 2024 for 2420円. It later debuted in western countries in the [[Drop Attack Battle Set]] released by [[Hasbro]].

It is the successor to [[CobaltDrake 4-60F]] and is succeeded by [[ShelterDrake 7-80GP]] in the toyline and its Balance custom, [[ImpactDrake 7-80GP]], in the anime. It is the right-spinning counterpart to [[MeteorDragoon 3-70J]].

==Trivia==
*ImpactDrake is the second of three Beys in [[Beyblade X (Season 2)|the second season of ''Beyblade X'']] to retain its original name in both the Japanese and English Versions of the anime; the others being [[AeroPegasus 3-70A|Aero Pegasus]] and [[ShelterDrake 7-80GP|Shelter Drake]].
*This is currently Khrome's only ''Drake'' Beyblade without a metal coat.
