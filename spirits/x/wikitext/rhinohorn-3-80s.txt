}}{{Translation|'''RhinoHorn 3-80S'''|ライノホーン{{Ruby|3-80|スリーエイティー}}{{Ruby|S|スパイク}}|RainoHōn Surī Eitī Supaiku|RhinoHorn Three Eighty Spike}} is a Defense Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Booster in Japan on November 2nd, 2023 for 1400円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Horn Rhino 3-80S''' in a Booster Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$9.99 in the United States.

In the toyline, it is succeeded by [[RhinoReaper C4-55D]].
