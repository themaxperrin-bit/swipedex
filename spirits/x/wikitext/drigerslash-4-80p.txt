It was released as a Booster in Japan on April 27th, 2024 for 1600円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Driger Slash 4-80P''' in a Starter Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$12.99 in the United States.

As part of the [[X-Over Project]], it is the ''[[Beyblade X]]'' remake of the [[Spin Gear System]] Beyblade, [[Driger Slash]].
