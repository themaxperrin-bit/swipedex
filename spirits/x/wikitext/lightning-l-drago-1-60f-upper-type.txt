It was released in Japan on September 14th, 2024 for 1600円 as one of the two prize Beyblades in [[Random Booster Lightning L-Drago 1-60F]].

As part of the [[X-Over Project]], it is the ''[[Beyblade X]]'' remake of the [[Hybrid Wheel System]] Beyblade, [[Lightning L-Drago 100HF]] in its "Upper Mode".

==Trivia==
*Lighting L-Drago was the top Metal Fight Beyblade choice in a poll held from August 7 to August 13, 2023 to select the next Beys for the X Series X-Over Project.
*The 1-60 Ratchet is chosen to mimic the 100 Spin Track in writing.
*The Flat Bit is chosen to mimic the Hole Flat Performance Tip.
*[[WizardArrow 4-80B]] is used to represent [[Flame Sagittario C145S]] on the box.
