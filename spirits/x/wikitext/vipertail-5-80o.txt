}}{{Translation|'''ViperTail 5-80O'''|ヴァイパーテイル{{Ruby|5-80|ファイブエイティー}}{{Ruby|O|オーブ}}|VaipāTeiru Faibu Eitī Ōbu|ViperTail Five Eighty Orb}} is a Stamina Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released in Japan on October 7th, 2023 for 1400円 as the prize Beyblade in [[Random Booster ViperTail Select]]. It later debuted in western countries as '''Tail Viper 5-80O''' as part of the [[Tail Viper and Sword Dran Dual Pack]] released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$14.99 in the United States.
