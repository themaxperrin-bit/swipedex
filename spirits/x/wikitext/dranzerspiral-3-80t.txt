}}{{Translation|'''DranzerSpiral 3-80T'''|ドランザースパイラル3-80T|DoranzāSupairaru 3-80T|DranzerSpiral Three Eighty Taper}} is a Balance Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Booster in Japan on July 15th, 2023 for 1600円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Dranzer Spiral 3-80T''' by [[Hasbro]] in Starter Packs with different color variations. It was released for USD$12.99 in the United States.

As part of the [[X-Over Project]], it is the ''[[Beyblade X]]'' remake of the [[Spin Gear System]] Beyblade, [[Dranzer Spiral]].

==Trivia==
*The Takara Tomy BX-00 Black Version release was first available at official [[Beyblade Battle Base|Beyblade Battle Base (B4)]] G1 and G2 events. It was later re-released as part of the [[Rare Bey Get Battle]] in Japan on October 10th, 2024. Through the [[Beyblade X (app)|''Beyblade X'' app]], Bladers have a 3% chance of obtaining a "Limited Edition Purchase Ticket", which can be then redeemed with the local distributor.
**Some Asian B4 stores also stocked this as a regular release, similar to releases like [[SharkEdge 5-60GF]].
*Hasbro's red F9584 release is possibly based on the SB-02 [[Dranzer S]] (Suzaku Color Ver.) from Takara's [[Bey Booster S]].
