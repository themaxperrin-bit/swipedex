}}{{Translation|'''PhoenixFeather 3-60F'''|フェニックスフェザー{{Ruby|3-60|スリーシクスティー}}{{Ruby|F|フラット}}|FenikkusuFezā Surī Shikusutī Furatto|PhoenixFeather Three Sixty Flat}} is an Attack Type Beyblade that appears in the ''[[Beyblade X]]'' anime and manga. In the anime and manga, it was originally created by [[Yggdrasil Inc.]] for [[Blaze Fujiwara]], and was subsequently mass-produced for the commercial market. It is succeeded by Blaze's [[PhoenixWing 9-60GF]].

The toy can be created with the [[Blade - PhoenixFeather|PhoenixFeather]] Blade released in the January 2024 issue of the [[CoroCoro Comic]] magazine, and the red [[Ratchet - 3-60|3-60]] Ratchet and [[Bit - Flat|Flat]] Bit from Takara Tomy's [[Battle Entry Set]] or Hasbro's [[Tail Viper and Sword Dran Dual Pack]] or [[Tackle Goat and Sword Dran Dual Pack]].<ref>https://youtu.be/pbKqZsWXSdE</ref>
