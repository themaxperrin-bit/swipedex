}}{{Translation|'''HellsChain 5-60HT'''|ヘルズチェイン{{Ruby|5-60|ファイブシクスティー}}{{Ruby|HT|ハイテーパー}}|HeruzuChein Faibu Shikusutī Hai Tēpā|HellsChain Five Sixty High Taper}} is a Balance Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a part of the [[HellsChain Deck Set]] in Japan on November 2nd, 2023 for 3960円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Chain Incendio 5-60HT''' as part of the [[Chain Incendio and Arrow Wizard Dual Pack]] released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$14.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[HellsScythe 4-60T]] and is succeeded by [[HellsHammer 3-70H]].
