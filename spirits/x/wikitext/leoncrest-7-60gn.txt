}}{{Translation|'''LeonCrest 7-60GN'''|レオンクレスト{{Ruby|7-60|セブンシクスティー}}{{Ruby|GN|ギヤニードル}}|ReonKuresuto Sebun Shikusutī Giya Nīdoru|LeonCrest Seven Sixty Gear Needle}} is a Defense Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Booster in Japan on August 10th, 2024 for 1400円. It was released by [[Hasbro]] as '''Crest Leon 7-60GN''' as part of the [[Cowl Sphinx and Crest Leon Dual Pack]] for USD$14.99 in the United States.

It is the successor to [[LeonClaw 5-60P]] and is succeeded by [[Fang Leon T 4-60U|LeonFang T4-60U]] in the toyline.
