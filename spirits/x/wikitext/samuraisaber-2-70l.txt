}}{{Translation|'''SamuraiSaber 2-70L'''|サムライセイバー{{Ruby|2-70|ツーセブンティー}}{{Ruby|L|レベル}}|SamuraiSeibā Tsū Sebuntī Reberu|SamuraiSaber Two Seventy Level}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Starter in Japan on November 2nd, 2024. It later debuted in western countries as '''Saber Samurai 2-70L''' in the '''Victory Grip Launcher Set''' released by [[Hasbro]]<!-- for USD$TBA in the United States-->.

It is the successor to [[SamuraiSteel 5-70GF]] and is succeeded by [[SamuraiCalibur 6-70M]].
