}}{{Translation|'''Mosasaurus 9-60U'''|モササウルス{{Ruby|9-60|ナインシクスティー}}{{Ruby|U|ユナイト}}|Mosasaurusu Nain Shikusutī Yunaito|Mosasaurus Nine Sixty Unite}} is a Balance Type Beyblade released by [[Hasbro]]. It was released as a part of the [[T. Rex and Mosasaurus Multipack Set]] for USD$24.99 in the United States. It later debuted as a part of the same set released by [[Takara Tomy]] on July 19th, 2025 for 4180円 in Japan.

The Takara Tomy release refers to this Beyblade's full name as '''Mosasaurus TriceraSpiky 9-60U Jurassic World Version'''.
