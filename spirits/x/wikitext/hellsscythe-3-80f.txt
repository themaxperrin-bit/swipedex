}}{{Translation|'''HellsScythe 3-80F'''|ヘルズサイズ3-80F|HeruzuSaizu 3-80F|HellsScythe Three Eighty Flat}} or {{Translation|'''SP X Bey'''|スペシャルクロスベイ|Supesharu Kurosu Bei|Special Cross Bey}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]] in Japan for 1750円. It can be obtained with a [[wikipedia:Mail order|mail order]] postcard included with the February 2024 issue of the [[CoroCoro Comic]] magazine, which released on January 15th, 2024.<ref>[https://corocoro.jp/news/377976/ CoroCoro Online Post about HellsScythe 3-80F]</ref> The first batch of orders were shipped on January 27th, 2024. The deadline for the mail order was February 17th, 2024.<ref>CoroCoro Comic February 2024 Issue, mail order postcard</ref>

The coloring is based on Team [[Persona]]'s colors and the Gear Chip design is by ''Beyblade X'' manga artist [[wikipedia:Posuka Demizu|Posuka Demizu]]. Included with the Beyblade is a "dress-up" sticker sheet and an illustration card.

In the manga, the Beyblade is owned by [[Robin Kazami]], who created it with the help of [[Jaxon Cross]] and [[Multi Nana-iro]]. It is intended to represent the friendship of Team Persona, and contains a part from each of their first Beys when they met.<ref>CoroCoro Comic January 2024 Issue, page 40</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Beyblade incorporates a part from each member of Team Persona:
**Robin's [[Blade - HellsScythe|HellsScythe]] Blade from [[HellsScythe 4-60T]].
**Multi's [[Ratchet - 3-80|3-80]] Ratchet from [[KnightShield 3-80N]].
**Jaxon's [[Bit - Flat|Flat]] Bit from [[DranSword 3-60F]].
*On the cover of chapter 8 of the manga, the members of Persona can be seen in a "Bey customization meeting" that resulted in this Bey.
