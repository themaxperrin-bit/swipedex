}}{{Translation|'''KnightShield 3-80N'''|ナイトシールド{{Ruby|3-80|スリーエイティー}}{{Ruby|N|ニードル}}|NaitoShīrudo Surī Eitī Nīdoru|KnightShield Three Eighty Needle}} is a Defense Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]]. It was released as a Starter in Japan on July 15th, 2023 for 1980円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Helm Knight 3-80N''' in a Starter Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$9.99 in the United States.

KnightShield would later get a ''Version 2.0'' release, which released in Japan as a part of the BX-53 [[All In One Set]] on October 10th, 2026 for 7480円.

While the primary version is owned by [[Multi Nana-iro]] in the ''[[Beyblade X]]'' anime and manga, recolored generic versions also appear in the anime. KnightShield's ''Version 2.0'' release would later debut in the anime/manga series, which would be developed by the [[Bey Kingdom]].

It is succeeded by [[KnightLance 4-80HN]].
