}}{{Translation|'''SamuraiSteel 5-70GF'''|サムライスチール{{Ruby|5-70|ファイブセブンティー}}{{Ruby|GF|ギヤフラット}}|SamuraiSuchīru Faibu Sebunti Giya Furatto|SamuraiSteel Five Seventy Gear Flat}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Takara Tomy]] in Japan on June 15th, 2025 for 2050円. It can be obtained with a [[wikipedia:Mail order|mail order]] postcard included with the June 2025 issue of the [[CoroCoro Comic]] magazine, which released on May 15th, 2025.

Although not shown in the manga, it appears in the May 2025 issue of the [[CoroCoro Comic]] magazine with a brief backstory: it is the predecessor of [[SamuraiSaber 2-70L]], and another heirloom Bey crafted by the [[Shiroboshi School]].<ref>CoroCoro Comic May 2025 Issue, page 23</ref>
