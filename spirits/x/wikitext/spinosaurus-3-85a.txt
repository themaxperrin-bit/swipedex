}}{{Translation|'''Spinosaurus 3-85A'''|スピノサウルス{{Ruby|3-85|スリーエイティーファイブ}}{{Ruby|A|アクセル}}|Supinosaurusu Surī Eitī Faibu Akuseru|Spinosaurus Three Eighty Five Accel}} is an Attack Type Beyblade released by [[Hasbro]]. It was released as a part of the [[Spinosaurus and Quetzalcoatlus Multipack Set]] for USD$24.99 in the United States. It later debuted as a part of the same set released by [[Takara Tomy]] on July 19th, 2025 for 4180円 in Japan.

The Takara Tomy release refers to this Beyblade's full name as '''Spinosaurus TyrannoRoar 3-85A Jurassic World Version'''.
