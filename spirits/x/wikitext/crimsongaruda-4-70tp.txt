It was released as a Booster in Japan on November 2nd, 2024 for 1400円. It later debuted in western countries as '''Scarlet Garuda 4-70TP''' in a Starter Pack released by [[Hasbro]] for USD$9.99 in the United States.

==Trivia==
*While CrimsonGaruda's name comes from [[wikipedia:Garuda|Garuda]], a bird-like divine creature from Hindu, Buddhist and Jain mythology, the gear chip and 4X avatar features the [[wikipedia:Vermillion Bird|Vermilion Bird]], one of the [[wikipedia:Four Symbols|Four Auspicious Beasts]] from Chinese mythology.
